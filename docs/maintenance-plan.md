## Why each item matters

- **Test smoke & CO alarms** — Working alarms cut the risk of dying in a home fire by more than half, and carbon monoxide has no smell. Test every alarm monthly.
- **Clean the dryer exhaust vent** — Lint that slips past the trap builds up inside the exhaust pipe — a leading cause of dryer fires. A newly bought house rarely comes with a cleaning record, so do the first one soon.
- **Clean the dryer lint trap** — A clean screen keeps air moving, dries clothes faster and keeps lint out of the vent. Ideally every load — this is the weekly nudge to make it a habit.
- **Clean the dishwasher filter** — Most modern dishwashers have a manual filter. When it clogs, dishes come out gritty and the machine starts to smell or drain slowly.
- **Deep clean the dishwasher** — Every 1–3 months depending on use. Clearing the spray arms, door gasket and drain keeps it cleaning well and heads off leaks and odors. Starts at every 2 months — adjust it to your usage.
- **Check the heat pump filter** — A dirty filter chokes airflow: the system works harder, efficiency drops and the coil can freeze in cooling season. Check monthly while the system is heating or cooling.
- **Replace the heat pump filter** — Disposable filters lose effectiveness even when they look fine. Replace every 1–3 months — sooner with pets, allergies or renovation dust. Starts at every 3 months; the monthly check tells you if it should be sooner.
- **Clear around the outdoor heat pump unit** — The outdoor unit needs free airflow. Leaves, grass clippings, snow and ice cut efficiency and can damage the fan or coil.
- **Book the heat pump tune-up** — Twice a year — before cooling season in spring and heating season in fall — a technician checks refrigerant charge, wiring, the condensate drain and the defrost cycle. It protects the warranty and catches problems before the coldest week of the year.
- **Flush the hot water tank** — Sediment settles at the bottom of the tank, making it noisy and less efficient and shortening its life. A yearly flush — plus a relief-valve test — keeps it safe.
- **Inspect the garage door & seals** — A yearly look at the door mechanism, auto-reverse and weather seals prevents injuries and keeps drafts, water and pests out of the lower level.
- **Mow the lawn** — Weekly in peak growth, every other week when it slows — change the frequency anytime. Mowing high (about 3 in / 7–8 cm) shades out weeds and helps grass survive dry spells.
- **Seasonal gutter & exterior check** — Clogged gutters overflow against the foundation — the most common cause of basement leaks. A quick check each season catches problems while they are cheap to fix.
- **Winterize the outdoor faucets** — Water left in an outdoor faucet line can freeze, split the pipe inside the wall and flood the basement when it thaws. Do it before the first hard frost.
- **Degrease the range hood filter** — Grease trapped in the hood filter is fuel sitting right above the stove. Clean it every 3 months — monthly if you fry often.
- **Check the kitchen fire extinguisher** — Every home should have an ABC extinguisher near the kitchen exit. If there isn't one yet, this is the reminder to buy one.
- **Replace smoke & CO alarm batteries** — Once a year when the clocks change in the fall — unless your alarms have sealed 10-year batteries.
- **Test GFCI outlets** — Ground-fault outlets in the kitchen, bathroom, garage and outside cut power in a fraction of a second when current leaks — but they can fail silently.
- **Clean the bathroom exhaust fan** — The fan pulls shower moisture out before it becomes mold and peeling paint. A dust-clogged fan moves a fraction of the air it should.
- **Inspect the tub & shower caulking** — Cracked or moldy caulk lets water behind the tile and into the ceiling below — expensive, invisible damage.
- **Check washer hoses & clean the washer** — A burst washing-machine hose can release hundreds of litres an hour. Catch bulges and cracks early and keep the drum fresh.
- **Vacuum the refrigerator coils** — Dusty condenser coils make the compressor run hot and long, wasting energy and shortening the fridge's life.
- **Test the house for radon** — Radon is an invisible, odorless soil gas and the leading cause of lung cancer in non-smokers. Testing is the only way to know your level — ideally a 3-month test during the heating season.
- **Check door & window weatherstripping** — Drafty doors and windows are among the cheapest energy fixes, and they cause cold spots that confuse the thermostat.
- **Find & label the main water shutoff** — In a leak, every minute counts. Both of you should know exactly where the main shutoff is — and that it actually turns.
- **Rekey or replace the exterior locks** — There's no telling how many keys previous owners, contractors or agents still have. Rekeying is inexpensive and a standard first step in a new home.
- **Map & label the electrical panel** — Accurate breaker labels make every repair, outage or emergency faster and safer.
- **Collect appliance manuals & warranties** — Model numbers, warranty dates and manuals turn future repairs into a two-minute lookup.
